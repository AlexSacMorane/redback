This configuration presents the dissolution of a source mineral, the diffusion of the solute, and the precipitation of a product mineral. The dissolution/precipitation phenomenon is dictated by the tilting of a free energy function. The amplitude of this tilting is controled by the concentration of a reactive specimen that diffuses in the domain.

last update: 06/29/2026
authors: Alexandre Sac-Morane, alexandre.sac-morane@enpc.fr

	- MOOSE outputs ("Case3_DissolutionDiffusionPrecipitation.mp4" and "Case3_DissolutionDiffusionPrecipitation_csv.csv")

The resolution is conducted with the module "Phase Field" of MOOSE
